🧮 Welcome to Sheet Lab!

This is your practice spreadsheet. Here's everything you need to get started:

Cells — every box has an address, like B3 (column B, row 3). Click a cell to select it.
Type data — click a cell and start typing. Press Enter to save and move down, Tab to move right.
Formulas — start with = to do math. Try =A1+A2 or =SUM(A1:A5).
Other handy formulas: =AVERAGE(A1:A5), =MIN(...), =MAX(...), =COUNT(...), =IF(A1>10,"big","small"), =ROUND(A1,2)
Select a range — click and drag across cells, or click one cell then shift-click another.
Format — use the toolbar for bold, colors, alignment, merging cells, and number styles.
Charts — select your data (include a header row!) and tap 📊 Chart.
Save — tap 💾 Save to keep your sheet on this device, or ⬇ CSV to download it.
